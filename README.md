## ⚡ About Me

I am a backend-focused Software Engineer and AI Engineer who designs production-grade intelligent systems. Having graduated with a B.Tech in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering from Symbiosis Institute of Technology (SIT), Pune in June 2026, I build scalable APIs, document intelligence workflows, and data orchestration pipelines. 

Currently, I work as a **Software Engineering Intern at Luminosity Software Limited**, developing backend AI capabilities. In parallel, I operate as a **Freelance Software Engineer**, building full-stack web applications and automated data analytics pipelines for clients.

### 📐 Engineering Philosophy
* **Simplicity first:** Start with the simplest modular architecture that satisfies requirements, allowing it to evolve naturally as the product scales.
* **Maintainability over Premature Optimization:** Prioritize writing clean, readable, and well-structured code. Good software is easily understood and extended by other developers.
* **Tool-to-Problem Fit:** Focus on understanding business workflows first, selecting database, framework, and AI models based on actual technical trade-offs.
